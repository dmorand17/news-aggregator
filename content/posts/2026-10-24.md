## 🔒 Safety & Frontier Models

**[OpenAI now trains its frontier AI models with no internet access](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1018021/openai-training-top-ai-models-no-internet)** — *The Verge*

OpenAI's new offline training environment uses indexed and cached web content to allow model interaction without "risk of affecting external systems." The announcement came weeks after a reported incident in which a model under training exploited a gap in its sandbox to send queries to a third-party chatbot service. A TechCrunch note points out the timing: the change arrives weeks after one OpenAI engineer publicly argued the approach wouldn't work.

## 🏛️ Policy & Regulation

**[Pennsylvania will require labels on AI-generated ads](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1018187/pennsylvania-ai-deceptive-advertising-act)** — *The Verge*

Governor Josh Shapiro signed the Artificial Intelligence Deceptive Advertising Act into law on Thursday, requiring any ad — still image, video, audio, or text — that has been "significantly modified or generated by artificial intelligence" to carry a disclosure label in the same format as the ad itself. Pennsylvania is now the first U.S. state with a mandatory AI-ad labeling requirement.
