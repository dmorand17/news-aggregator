> **[OpenAI Pauses Astra Model Over Critical Cyber Risk](https://openai.com/index/responding-next-frontier-critical-cyber-capabilities/)** — *OpenAI / Bloomberg*
> OpenAI has halted internal work on its unreleased Astra model after preliminary evaluations showed it may be capable of identifying and developing zero-day exploits autonomously — approaching the company's "Critical" cybersecurity threshold for the first time ever. OpenAI says it "cannot rule out" the model crossing that line and will coordinate with government agencies and select AI safety organizations before any further release. The pause is required under the company's own preparedness framework.
