## 🧮 AI Does Science

**[A Claude-assisted proof of the Riemann Hypothesis clears its first independent checks](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/20/ai-claimed-a-solution-to-the-167-year-old-riemann-hypothesis-and-a-human-did-too/)** — *TechCrunch*

Anthropic mathematician Levent Alpöge published a four-page note Friday claiming he and a non-public version of Claude had proved the 167-year-old Riemann Hypothesis, shipping a 17-million-line Lean formalization for verification. Within hours, MIT's Andrew Sutherland had compiled all 175,187 modules with zero errors, confirmed the five headline theorems depend only on the three standard axioms, and saw two independently written kernels accept the export — though the statement-matching comparator run was still in progress at publication. In an unusual twist, a 940-page human proof of a generalized version of the hypothesis surfaced the same day; the Clay Institute's two-year scrutiny rule still applies either way.

[Verification files for the Lean proof of the Riemann Hypothesis](https://math.mit.edu/~drew/grh/) — *Andrew Sutherland, MIT* — A live table of every check run against the submission, including multi-kernel replays and the remaining comparator work.

**[GPT-6 Astra helps formulate the Standard Model on a lattice](https://openai.com/index/step-toward-defining-the-standard-model/)** — *OpenAI*

Physicist Nathaniel Craig worked with GPT-6 Astra on a candidate lattice formulation of the Standard Model, a longstanding problem that normally requires perturbative approximations. Astra helped construct the mathematical framework and a detailed proof supporting the approach.

**[OpenAI models cut chip area by 10–13%](https://openai.com/index/advancing-chip-design-with-our-models/)** — *OpenAI*

Design changes proposed by OpenAI's models reduced circuit area on key parts of its Jalapeño AI chip by an estimated 10% to 13%. Working with AMD, the same approach delivered improvements of up to 14% on flagship GPU designs.

[Claude was trained on protein sequences at scale](https://xinmingtu.cn/blog/2026/claude-opus-5-5-protein-sequences/) — *Xinming Tu* — The latest Claude models can predict the next amino acid for little-studied bacterial proteins and fail on shuffled sequences, a capability no other tested model showed.
