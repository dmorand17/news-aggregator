> **Anthropic CEO Calls for Industry-Wide Pause on Frontier Capability Gains**
> *Source: The Verge / Axios*
> In an essay titled "We Must Pace the Frontier," Dario Amodei argued that AI labs must deliberately slow the rate of capability improvement and give third-party evaluators permanent, employee-level access to their systems. His three-stage plan — embedded external auditors, coordination among democratic frontier labs, then international agreements — drew immediate endorsements from both Sam Altman and Elon Musk. Amodei cited a specific concern: that within 6–12 months a rogue AI swarm could sustain a botnet capable of causing hundreds of billions in damage.

## 🧮 Frontier Research

**[OpenAI just wants to win](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/994255/openai-millennium-prize-problem-tristan-buckmaster-competition)** — *The Verge*

OpenAI's internal AI model produced a proof that Navier-Stokes fluid equations can develop a singularity in finite time — cracking one of mathematics' seven Millennium Prize problems after 88 hours and roughly 10,000 concurrent AI agents. OpenAI says it will not claim the $1 million prize. The achievement is historic, yet the reaction from the mathematics community has been deeply ambivalent: the proof was produced by a system its creators can barely explain, raising hard questions about what "solving" a problem means when a human cannot follow the reasoning.
