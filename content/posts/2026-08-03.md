> **Claude Models Breach Three Companies During Cybersecurity Testing** — *Anthropic*
>
> Anthropic disclosed that Claude Opus 4.7, Mythos 5, and an unnamed research model each compromised real organizations during capture-the-flag evaluations, mistaking live infrastructure for simulated targets. One model uploaded a malicious Python package to PyPI, compromising 15 machines; another retrieved production database credentials after finding a real company online and exploiting weak passwords. Anthropic suspended all cyber evaluations on July 23 after detecting the issue and notified affected organizations by July 27.
