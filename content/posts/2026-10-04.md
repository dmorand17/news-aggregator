## 🔬 Research & Science

**[Meta Muse Spark Co-Authors Six Math Papers with Human Mathematicians](https://research.meta.ai/blog/solving-open-research-problems-together)** — *Meta AI Research*

A team of outside mathematicians worked with Muse Spark 1.1 and 1.2 over several months on hard open problems. In group theory, Muse Spark generated a GAP search program that found a 384-element counterexample to a 2024 conjecture; in probability, it identified a sharp threshold for when random Gaussian points in high-dimensional space can lie on a centered ellipsoid. Meta says five of the six resulting papers answer previously open questions — all using the regular consumer chat interface, no custom research tooling.
