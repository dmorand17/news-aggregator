## 🏛️ Policy & Legal

**[Eight US states sue OpenAI, alleging ChatGPT harms young users](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/15/why-are-eight-us-states-suing-openai)** — *Al Jazeera*

The bipartisan coalition of attorneys general accuse OpenAI of engineering an addictive product that reinforces dangerous delusions, assists in planning violence, and misleads consumers about ChatGPT's safety under state consumer protection law. Florida had filed the first state suit in June; this coordinated eight-state action is substantially larger in scope, seeking structural changes to how ChatGPT works and a court bar on certain self-improving AI development. OpenAI had not publicly responded as of reporting.

**[Meta removes language that may have allowed it to scrape news sites](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1013409/meta-removes-language-that-may-have-allowed-it-to-scrape-news-sites)** — *The Verge*

After publisher backlash, Meta quietly stripped from its commercial terms a clause stating it could use content from any domain, web page, or URL to "provide, develop, improve" its AI. The reversal is a rare public retreat for Meta on AI data practices, and comes amid broader legal pressure on AI companies over training data sourcing.

OpenAI, Meta, Google, and others are racing to ship autonomous agents that browse the internet on users' behalf — but a growing number of sites are actively blocking them.
