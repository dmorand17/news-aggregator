Simultaneously, the White House convened leaders from Google, Meta, OpenAI, Nvidia, Anthropic, and xAI to sign a parallel voluntary safety accord Trump called "morally binding." ## 🏛️ Policy & Governance

**[Trump Signs 'Super Intelligence' Executive Order, Tech Titans Ink Separate SI Accord](https://www.foxbusiness.com/politics/trump-signs-executive-order-rebranding-ai-super-intelligence-tech-titans-ink-separate-accord)** — *Fox Business*

The executive order requires agencies to cease using "AI" in applicable settings and adopt "Super Intelligence" language across new reports, contracts, and web properties — though it does not require revising existing regulations or historical documents. The accompanying White House accord, signed by executives from the six largest AI players, is voluntary but represents the most visible government-industry alignment on AI governance to date. The rebrand appears driven by techno-nationalist framing, asserting US autonomy in a zero-sum competition for global tech leadership.

**[Apple Says It's Tightening macOS 'Full Disk Access' Controls Due to New Risks From AI Agents](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/02/apple-says-its-tightening-macos-full-disk-access-controls-due-to-new-risks-from-ai-agents/)** — *TechCrunch*

Apple is rolling out new controls for the Full Disk Access permission that restrict how apps can gain access to files, messages, mail, and browsing history, citing a "substantial" increase in risk from increasingly capable AI agents. The change requires users who genuinely wish to grant extraordinary access to go through a more deliberate confirmation flow, raising the bar above what an ambient agent can silently acquire.

[arXiv's Updated Rate Limit Policy](https://blog.arxiv.org/2026/10/01/updated-rate-limit-policy/) — *arXiv Blog*
