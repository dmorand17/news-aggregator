Sony Music Publishing, Warner Chappell Music, and affiliated publishers filed suit against Anthropic, CEO Dario Amodei, and co-founder Benjamin Mann on August 28, alleging a "brazen campaign" of IP theft. The complaint claims Anthropic illegally torrented, scraped, and downloaded thousands of copyrighted songs — including works by Taylor Swift, Marvin Gaye, and Survivor — to train Claude, and that the model can reproduce lyrics on demand in ways that undermine licensed lyric services. Plaintiffs are seeking up to $150,000 per willfully infringed work. The suit cites a 2025 finding that Anthropic downloaded over 7 million pirated books from Library Genesis as evidence of a pattern.
