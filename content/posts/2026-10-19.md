## 🤖 AI Agents & Developer Tools

**[Redesigning source control at Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/redesigning-source-control)** — *Claude Blog*

Anthropic's monorepo merge rate exploded from ~2,500 to over 30,000 commits per day between May and September 2026 — driven largely by always-online AI agents. Git couldn't keep up, so a seven-person team built Scion, an in-house version control system designed from scratch for network-connected agent workloads. The migration off git took five months; Scion delivers a 200x smaller CPU/memory footprint than the previous git-mirroring infrastructure and dramatically faster checkouts for CI and research jobs. Anthropic has no plans to open-source it but says it wants to bring some ideas back to git via GitHub, GitLab, and the open-source community.
